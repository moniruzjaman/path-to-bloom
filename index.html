Abundance is national. The gap is local.

Part III · The Path Forward

Four layers. Every one already owned by the government.

The reform asks for no miracle technology — only that assets already in hand be connected into one transparent, auditable circuit from farmer identity to verified outcome.

Krishak Card (launched Apr 2026 · 120 data points · 20,671 farmers receiving Tk 2,500/yr · 22M IDs by 2028) + NID + BDS/EDLMS (20,000+ mouza maps integrated, 138,000 digitizing, Korea MoU Sep 2025). Biometric linkage to verified land parcels and crop declarations.
Biometric PoS devices at dealer shops — Aadhaar-style, no-denial authentication with offline capability and local server sync.
Central Digital Ledger with blockchain verification: an immutable record from import or production through dealer to farmer, exposed on a public dashboard.
National Digital Agriculture Platform + satellite NDVI: mobile-first soil-health advice and independent verification of crop declarations.

Part V · Implementation

Sixty months. Four phases. One direction.

Each capability is proven before the next depends on it.

  1. Months 1–12

    I · Foundation

    • Dealer Policy implementing rules with digital compliance mandates
    • Biometric PoS pilots in 2 divisions; local server nodes in pilot unions
    • Krishak Card ↔ NID + initial BDS parcels; Soil Health Card sampling
    • KrishiChain Phase 1 — 50 dealers, $219K, 95% transaction capture
  2. Months 13–24

    II · Integration

    • Nationwide biometric PoS + e-Bill rollout on the India model
    • Quotas linked to verified land area & crop type; mobile pre-booking app
    • Satellite NDVI verification for Boro; public transparency dashboard
    • KrishiChain Phase 2 — $850K, 20% of diverted value recovered
  3. Months 25–36

    III · Optimization

    • e-Voucher pilots move subsidy from companies to farmers over MFS rails
    • 1M+ Soil Health Cards; AI/ML demand forecasting; BADC MIS integrated
    • Import share falls from 70% toward 50%
    • KrishiChain Phase 3 — $1.85M, 5,000 dealers, +10% nationwide NUE
  4. Months 37–60

    IV · Sustainability

    • Full transition to targeted, land-linked entitlements
    • Market linkages rewarding sustainable practice; independent oversight body
    • Full alignment with SDG 2.4 & FAO Better Production
    • South Asian agricultural-governance leadership secured
